# Neural_Scribbes🧠

A personal knowledge repository of handwritten notes on Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Computer Vision.

Most of these notes are scribbled while preparing and delivering lectures to my students, discussing ideas on a whiteboard, or working through mathematical derivations myself. They reflect my journey of learning, teaching, and researching modern AI.



## Purpose

This repository serves as my personal knowledge base and aims to help students, researchers, and practitioners explore the mathematical and conceptual foundations of modern AI.
